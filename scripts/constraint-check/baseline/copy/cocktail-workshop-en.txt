Cocktail workshop in Maastricht
Book a cocktail workshop in Maastricht at Cliniq on Platielstraat. Available Thursday, Friday and Saturday, starting between 19:00 and 20:30. Stay on for the club night after.
Groups from 15. Every guest makes at least 3 cocktails (€15 each) guided by our bartenders. No experience needed.

Is a cocktail workshop good for a hen party in Maastricht?
Yes: the cocktail workshop at Cliniq Maastricht is one of the most popular hen-party activities in the city. Groups of 15 or more make cocktails together, guided by the bartenders at Platielstraat 9A, then stay on for the regular club night. Price starts at €15 per cocktail.
FAQ
Frequently asked questions
How much does a workshop cost? €15 per cocktail, minimum 3 per person. No venue or bartender surcharge.
What is the minimum group size? 15 people minimum.
How do I book? Email contact@cafecliniq.com or send a WhatsApp. Share your date, group size and preferred start time.
Can we stay after the workshop? Yes — stay for the regular club night. Normal entry rules apply.
Which days are available? Thursday, Friday and Saturday. Start between 19:00 and 20:30.
Is it good for a hen party? Yes, one of our most-booked activities for hen nights. Exclusive use and full packages available.
