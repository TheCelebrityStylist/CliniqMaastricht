What makes a good event location in central Maastricht?

The space is available for exclusive private hire on Thursday, Friday and Saturday for up to 400 guests, with bar, lighting, sound and dancefloor already in place — suitable for corporate events, drinks and private parties.
FAQ
Frequently asked questions
What events can I organise at CLINIQ? CLINIQ works for company parties, birthdays, drinks, bachelor and bachelorette groups, student nights, presentations, product launches and private events. The venue already has a bar, lighting, sound and club atmosphere, so you are not starting with an empty room.
How many people does the venue hold? Capacity depends on the setup and type of event. Drinks, club nights and presentations all need a different layout. Share your expected guest count in the inquiry and we will advise on a suitable setup.
What is included with venue hire? This depends on the request. It can include use of the space, bar, basic lighting, sound and staff. Extras such as a DJ, cocktails, drinks packages or specific technical needs can be added to the proposal.
Is music, lighting and a DJ possible? Yes. CLINIQ is designed as a club and event venue, so music, lighting and sound are central to the space. If you bring your own DJ, we discuss the technical needs. If not, we can advise.
Can we arrange cocktails or drinks packages? Yes. You can work with regular bar sales, a drinks package or specific agreements for cocktails, bubbles or welcome drinks. Tell us what kind of group you are hosting and how you want the evening to run.
What does it cost to hire CLINIQ? Pricing depends on date, times, guest count, staffing, drinks agreements and technical extras. We work with tailored quotes so the proposal fits your event instead of forcing a standard package.
How do I request a quote? Use the form on the event space page. Include the date, guest count, type of event, preferred times and wishes for music, drinks or programme. With that information we can respond clearly.
